Scrambeled Eggs

Eggs are one of my favourite foods. Here is a recipe for deliciously rich scrambeled eggs.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1tbs butter
  • 2tbs cream

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a frying pan over a medium heat
  2. Gently mix the eggs and cream in a bowl
  3. Using a spactula fold the eggs form the eggs of the pan to the centre every 20 seconds(as if you are making an omelete)
  4. When the eggs are still moist remove from the heat(it will continue to cook on the plate till served)
